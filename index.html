Approach

What building six products alone with AI actually taught.

The products are the artifact. The method is the point. Four things held up across every release, and none of them are what the tooling vendors say. The full write-up is in Six Products, One Operator.

Where it holds

Scaffolding, boilerplate, refactors, test generation, and first drafts of anything structured. The speed gain here is real and it is large. Work that used to justify a team of three moved inside one person's week.

Where it breaks

Anything requiring judgment about what should be built rather than how. Architecture decisions, data model tradeoffs, pricing, and the question of whether a feature should exist at all. Fluent output is not the same as correct output, and the failure mode is confident and wrong.

Where review has to sit

Close to the decision, not at the end of the pipeline. Reviewing generated work after it has been integrated costs more than writing it manually. The gate belongs at the point of intent, before generation, and again at the boundary where the work touches something a user depends on.

Why adoption stalls

Not at the build. At the last mile, where the new way of working has to survive contact with existing process, existing quality standards, and people who are accountable for outcomes. Capability arrives long before behaviour changes.
